linkwarren, round 4

Six more directions, leaning harder into AI: gradients, orbits, bursts and solid forms. Each card shows the mark large on dark, small on light at 56, 32 and 16 pixels, and next to the name.

1. Route burst

Twelve routes radiating from one hub, ending at nodes of different reach. The hub is your short link, the nodes are every domain and destination. The same family of shapes as the AI-lab bursts, but drawn as a network.

linkwarren

2. Isometric block

A solid block with a tunnel opening on top and exits on two faces. Infrastructure you can almost hold: it reads as a piece of hardware sitting at the edge.

linkwarren

3. Orbit

A glowing core with two orbits and nodes riding them. The most overtly AI of the set: an engine with links circling it.

linkwarren

4. Stepped w

A w built from blocks, shading from light to deep orange left to right. Bold and pixel-precise, and it scales from favicon to billboard.

linkwarren

5. Forward

Two chevrons and a node: you arrive, you get sent onward. Fast, simple, and it doubles as a terminal prompt.

linkwarren

6. Endless loop

An infinity loop drawn as one tunnel, with a node at the far end. Links that never break, routes that never end.

linkwarren
