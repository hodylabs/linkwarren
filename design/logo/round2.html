linkwarren, round 2

More technical: graphs, circuit traces and network geometry, in graphite with your hodylabs orange as the signal colour. Each mark is shown on light and dark, then at 64, 32 and 16 pixels for favicon and avatar use.

1. Fan-out

A routing diagram. One orange input hits a router node and fans out to three endpoints, which is exactly what the app does with a short link and its domains.

2. Edge chip

A chip with one pin in and three pins out, the traces running through it. It nods to running at the edge and reads as infrastructure.

3. Hex link

Two hexagons locked together like chain links. Hexagons read as graph cells and network nodes, and the interlock says link without the usual paperclip icon.

4. Trace monogram

The letters l and w drawn as circuit traces, ending in vias. The w's low points double as tunnels in the warren.

With the wordmark

Unbounded, a wide geometric face, lowercase like the repo name. The wordmark stays one colour so the mark carries the orange.
